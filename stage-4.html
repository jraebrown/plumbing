Stage 4 of 10: Cable Scan & Drilling
04
Cable Scan & Drilling
⏱ 3 hrs Medium
🎯 Stage Goal:

Scan partition walls with Bosch Truvo scanner, mark skirting route, drill sleeved wall penetrations, and install cushioned clips every 600mm.

⚠️ Watch-out:
Always scan BOTH sides of partition walls before drilling! Wall penetration hole size is confirmed with plumber based on sleeve and insulation type.
✅ Complete When:
All partition wall penetrations drilled and fitted with protective sleeves, and rubber-lined cushioned pipe clips installed every 600mm along skirting.
⚠️ DO NOT USE UNLINED CLIPS: Rigid plastic clips cause loud clicking/ticking noises as copper expands when heated. Rubber-lined cushioned clips absorb movement silently.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Mark horizontal pipe centerline along skirting board using spirit level.
2
Scan BOTH sides of partition walls using Bosch Truvo scanner to detect hidden electric cables or metal studs.
3
Drill wall penetration holes at skirting level (hole size confirmed with plumber based on sleeve & insulation).
4
Insert protective wall sleeves into masonry/plasterboard penetrations.
5
Install rubber-lined 15mm cushioned pipe clips every 600mm along horizontal runs to allow smooth thermal expansion.
⚙️ Hole Size Rule: Penetration diameter is NOT automatically 22mm. Confirm exact bit size with plumber based on whether pipe insulation passes through wall or sleeves are used.
