04

Route and penetrations

Use this as a personal check that the agreed visible route has been marked, service risks have been considered and any approved penetrations have been completed appropriately.

Completion: Route and penetrations completed and verified, ready for the next steps.

Do not drill solely because a detector gives a clear reading. Hidden services and structural conditions require appropriate checks; stop where the wall, route or drilling method is uncertain.

Preparation and verification sequence

1Mark the proposed route and check it still matches the reviewed plan and available clearances.
2Identify potential services and construction risks using appropriate information and detection methods; obtain help if any result is unclear.
3Agree penetration positions, protection, fire-stopping implications and drilling method before making openings.
4Complete only the approved penetrations, protecting the pipe and surrounding construction as specified.
5Record the final route, penetrations and any deviations so they can be reviewed before pipework is installed.
6Confirm supports, movement allowance and access requirements with the plumber before fitting pipework.

Review note

Clip type, spacing, sleeve detail, penetration size, fire-stopping and route protection are project-specific. Confirm them before installation rather than using generic dimensions.
