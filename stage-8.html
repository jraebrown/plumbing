Stage 8: Boiler Connections

Next →
Qualified-work boundary: Do not work on gas components or boiler internals. The qualified engineer must identify the correct heating connections on site and carry out or directly control any appliance reconnection required by the manufacturer and applicable rules.

Purpose

Present the completed, visible heating pipework for reconnection. Do not use assumed flow/return labels, insertion depths, fitting types or quantities from a generic drawing.

More detail on engineer role

The engineer should positively identify each appliance connection, confirm the permitted reconnection method and fittings for that boiler and installation, and either perform the work or supervise it directly. Use this stage to capture what was agreed and done, not to specify your own boiler-side procedure.

Engineer review record

More detail on records

In the notes, record the engineer’s name and company, the boiler model, the connections used, any conditions they set for future changes, and how testing and refill should be carried out. This gives you and any future engineer a clear history of what was agreed at the appliance.

Complete when: Boiler connections completed and signed off by the qualified engineer.

Stage notes

DO NOT ALTER GAS COMPONENTS OR BOILER INTERNALS — use an appropriately qualified engineer
