Stage 8 of 10: Boiler Reconnection
08
Boiler Reconnection
⏱ 2 hrs HIGH RISK
🎯 Stage Goal:

Reconnect new 15mm flow and return trunks to verified boiler stubs above BoilerMag filter under direct engineer supervision.

⚠️ Watch-out:
STOP — ENGINEER MUST BE PRESENT! Do NOT touch gas (yellow handle) or DHW stubs. Do NOT cut pipes based on diagrams alone!
✅ Complete When:
Heating engineer positively identifies CH flow and return stubs on site; 15mm Tectite equal couplers fitted above BoilerMag filter.
🚨 GAS & BOILER MANDATE: Homeowner must NOT cut, loosen, or reconnect gas pipes or boiler internals. Final boiler connection MUST be completed or directly supervised by Gas Safe engineer.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Have qualified heating engineer positively identify CH Return stub (with BoilerMag filter) and CH Flow stub on site.
2
Clean old embedded stub ends with steel wool and deburr edges.
3
Mark 23mm insertion depth on boiler stubs.
4
Push 15mm Tectite Sprint equal couplers onto boiler stubs.
5
Connect new 15mm flow and return main trunks into couplers under engineer supervision.
6
Do NOT touch gas (yellow handle) or DHW stubs under any circumstances!
🔗 Coupler Specs: Tectite Sprint 15mm Equal Couplers (Screwfix Code: 5808G). Requires 8 units total for joining pipe lengths and boiler stubs.
