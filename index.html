Pre-Work Approvals & Mandatory Consents

TL;DR: Secure written freeholder consent, notify your home insurer, and get plumber approval before buying or cutting anything.

  • ✅ Freeholder Consent: Obtain formal written permission to alter above-surface pipework.
  • ✅ Insurer Notification: Email your insurer confirming DIY water-side work only; Gas Safe handles the boiler.
  • 🛑 Stop Condition: Do not order materials, drill, or cut until professional route approval is recorded.

First-Floor Flat Water-Escape Risk

Because you live above another flat, any leak poses a catastrophic risk to the property directly below. Proceeding without consent could breach your lease and invalidate your buildings insurance.

The Gas Safe Boundary

You can legally cut/join copper flow and return pipes. You cannot touch the gas supply pipe or disconnect the boiler's gas connection. Only a Gas Safe engineer can do this.

Stage 5 of 10

Push-Fit Preparation: The Golden Sequence

TL;DR: Every joint lives or dies by pipe preparation. Cut square, deburr, clean, mark depth, and push.

  • 🔧 1. Cut Square: Use the Rothenberger Pipeslice only. NEVER use a hacksaw.
  • 🔧 2. Deburr: Use a reamer on the inside and outside edge to prevent O-ring damage.
  • ⭐ 3. Clean & Inspect: Wipe the pipe end clean. Ensure it is round and scratch-free.
  • 📝 4. Mark Depth: Measure and mark the 23mm insertion depth with a pen.
  • ✅ 5. Push to Mark: Push firmly until the marker meets the collar, then pull-check.

Protecting the O-Ring

A hacksaw leaves a ragged, angled edge. When you push this edge into a Tectite Sprint fitting, it slices the internal rubber O-ring. It might hold pressure initially, but it will eventually fail. Behind boxing in a first-floor flat, this is a five-figure disaster.
