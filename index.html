Central Heating Replacement Plan

Proposed 15 mm two-pipe replacement — first-floor flat — Glow-worm Compact 24c-AS/1

PLANNING DOCUMENT — Not installation approval. All pipe sizes, routes, boiler connections, penetrations, fittings, test criteria and commissioning steps require on-site approval by an appropriately qualified heating engineer.

Non-negotiable hold points

  • Do not order pipe or fittings until the engineer confirms the system layout, radiator load, pipe diameter, pressure drop and pump capability.
  • Do not cut, loosen or reconnect any boiler-side pipe until the engineer positively identifies the central-heating flow and return connections.
  • Do not drill penetrations until wall construction, concealed services, sleeves, fire-stopping and acoustic requirements are confirmed.
  • Do not box in pipework until the agreed cold and hot leak/pressure tests have passed with no leakage.
  • Use the current fitting and boiler manufacturer instructions, plus the engineer's method, where they differ from this planning document.

Proposed circuit concept

This is a planning concept, not a verified as-built drawing. Two parallel mains are proposed: a flow main leaves the boiler and a return main returns to it. Radiators 1 and 2 are intended to connect independently between those mains.

At radiator 3, the flow main connects to one radiator valve and the return main connects to the other. The radiator bridges the two mains hydraulically; the return main then follows the parallel route back to the boiler. No direct flow-to-return link is proposed.
