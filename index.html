Pre-Work Approvals & Governance

TL;DR: Check lease terms, notify your insurer, consult Building Control, and confirm the provisional route before buying pipe.

  • ✅ Freeholder/Insurer: Review lease clauses for structural/service alterations. Notify your insurer in writing; proceeding without consent may affect policy coverage.
  • ✅ Building Regulations: Confirm with local Building Control or a Competent Person scheme installer whether heating distribution changes require notification.
  • 🛑 Stop Condition: Do not order materials, drill, or cut until professional route verification is complete.

Wet Work vs Gas Safe Boundary

HSE distinguishes between "wet work" (installing room-side copper water pipes, valves, and radiators) and "gas work" on the appliance itself. A competent person may install water-side pipework. However, opening the boiler casing, breaking combustion seals, touching gas pipework, or reconnecting the boiler requires an appropriately qualified Gas Safe engineer.

Route Measurement & Mapping

TL;DR: Measure room perimeters on site; never order pipe based solely on schematic floor plans.

  • ✅ On-Site Measuring: Record every vertical drop, skirting offset, and wall thickness directly with a tape measure.
  • ✅ Double the Run: Two-pipe circuits require separate flow (red) and return (blue) trunks along the entire perimeter.
  • ✅ Outside Diameter: Verify boiler stub OD using a vernier caliper (15mm OD = 15mm copper pipe).

Stage 3 of 10

Safe Draining & Isolation

TL;DR: Isolate power via approved procedures, confirm cooling, and drain safely to protect against scalding and sludge stains.

  • ✅ Electrical Safe Isolation: Follow safe electrical isolation procedures at the fused spur. If in doubt, use a qualified electrician or heating engineer.
  • ✅ Cooling Verification: Ensure the system and water have cooled fully before opening valves or cutting pipe.
  • ✅ Drainage Hose: Attach hose to drain valve, route to bucket/drain, and open bleed valves on upper radiators to prevent vacuum lock.
  • 🛑 Verify Depressurisation: The boiler pressure gauge must read exactly 0 bar before cutting into any copper run.

Black Magnetite Sludge

Old heating circuits contain thick, black iron oxide sludge and chemical inhibitors. This liquid stains carpets permanently. Have heavy towels and a wet/dry vacuum immediately ready near every cut point.

Stage 4 of 10

Route and Penetrations

TL;DR: Scan walls before drilling, sleeve all partition penetrations, and install cushioned clips.

  • ✅ Multi-Scanning: Scan BOTH sides of every partition wall for hidden electrical conduits and cables before drilling.
  • ✅ Wall Sleeving: Fit sleeves or grommets in all wall penetrations to protect copper tube and maintain fire separation.
  • ✅ Acoustic Clips: Agree clip spacing with installer (typically ~600mm horizontal) using rubber-lined cushioned clips to dampen thermal ticking.

Thermal Movement & Fire Stopping

Do not fix copper tube rigidly in masonry holes. Holes must allow for expansion sleeves. If penetrating fire-resisting partition walls, intumescent sealant or approved fire sleeves must be fitted.

Stage 5 of 10

Push-Fit Preparation: The Golden Sequence

TL;DR: Push-fit joints live or die by tube preparation. Cut square, deburr thoroughly, clean, mark insertion depth, and push.

  • 🔧 1. Cut Square: Use the Rothenberger Pipeslice only. NEVER use a hacksaw.
  • 🔧 2. Deburr: Use a reamer on the inside and outside edge to prevent O-ring damage.
  • ⭐ 3. Clean & Inspect: Wipe the pipe end clean. Ensure it is round and scratch-free.
  • 📝 4. Mark Depth: Confirm the socket depth from the instructions supplied with the exact fitting. (Standard 15mm Sprint is typically 16mm, but VERIFY before assembly).
  • ✅ 5. Push to Mark: Push firmly until the marker meets the collar, then pull-check.

Socket Depth Clarification

There is a critical difference between Tectite ranges that you must verify against your fitting packaging:

  • VSH Tectite Sprint (Non-demountable): Manufacturer published socket depth for standard 15mm Sprint is 16mm.
  • VSH Tectite Classic / Pro (Demountable): Socket depth is 23mm due to internal demounting ring collars.

Rule: Always confirm socket depth with a ruler against the fitting stop and the manufacturer's packaging slip before marking.

Stage 6 of 10

Radiator Branchwork

TL;DR: Connect radiators R1 and R2 in parallel using equal tees; trunks must continue past each radiator.

  • ✅ Continuous Mains: Trunks do NOT terminate at R1 or R2. Cut the mains and insert 15x15x15mm Tectite equal tees so flow continues.
  • ✅ Upward Branch Stubs: Run short vertical stubs up from the tee branch to the radiator valve tails.
  • 🛑 Confirm TRV Flow Side: Do not assume flow is on the left or right. The engineer must confirm TRV orientation on site.

Why Parallel, Never Series

In a two-pipe system, each radiator acts as an independent bridge between the hot flow main and cool return main. Connecting radiators in series causes downstream radiators to receive cooled water, destroying system efficiency.

Stage 7 of 10

Terminal Loop Closure (R3)

TL;DR: Terminate the loop at Bedroom 2 using 90° elbows (no tees); hand-trace the return back to the boiler.

  • ✅ Terminal Elbows: At Radiator R3, trunks terminate with 90° elbows turning directly up into the valves.
  • ✅ The Radiator Bridge: Never join the red flow and blue return pipes directly together; the radiator itself acts as the circuit bridge.
  • ✅ Hand-Trace Route: Physically follow the blue return trunk along perimeter walls to confirm an unbroken path to the boiler.

Visual Check Only

Visual inspection confirms mechanical layout and alignment; it does not prove leak tightness. Keep all pipework, joints, and fittings completely exposed and accessible for the engineer's testing phase.

Stage 8 of 10

Professional Reconnection

TL;DR: Present your capped, visible pipework stubs and hand over to the qualified Gas Safe engineer.

  • 🛑 Gas Safe Boundary: Do not open the boiler casing, break combustion seals, or touch the gas supply. Final appliance connections must be executed by a registered Gas Safe engineer.
  • ✅ Positive Pipe ID: The engineer identifies heating flow and return directly using appliance documentation.
  • ✅ Log Details: Record the engineer's name, Gas Safe license number, and agreed reconnection fittings.

Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations

DIY work is strictly limited to room-side wet pipework. Any operation touching the appliance block, flue, internal hydraulics, or gas-carrying parts is legally restricted to certified engineers.

Stage 9 of 10

Testing, Refill & Commissioning

TL;DR: Fill to operating pressure, execute specified pressure tests, wipe every joint with dry blue roll, and dose inhibitor.

  • ✅ Cold Fill vs Leak Test: Refill system via braided loop to engineer-specified operating pressure. Perform formal pressure test per engineer's required duration and pressure.
  • ⭐ Blue Roll Wipe Test: Wipe a piece of dry blue roll around EVERY push-fit and valve fitting. Any dampness = immediate failure.
  • ✅ Flush, Dose & Balance: Clean magnetic filter (BoilerMag), flush debris, introduce chemical inhibitor, and balance radiators.
  • 🛑 Do Not Conceal: Keep pipework fully visible throughout cold and hot observation cycles.

Comprehensive Commissioning Checklist

BS 7593 and Building Regulations Part L require full hydronic commissioning:

  • Flushing and magnetite cleansing.
  • BoilerMag magnetic filter inspection and cleaning.
  • Compatible chemical inhibitor dosing and concentration verification.
  • Hydraulic balancing across R1, R2, and R3 lockshield valves.
  • Monitoring gauge stability through cold and hot expansion cycles.

Stage 10 of 10

Aesthetic Boxing In

TL;DR: Hide the pipe runs with timber boxing, but retain permanent access hatches over all joints and valves.

  • ✅ Batten Frame: Secure 25x38mm timber battens to walls above and below the copper trunks.
  • ✅ 160mm Fascia: Fit 160mm wide MDF strips (leaves clearance for future services and thermal airflow).
  • ✅ Thermal Expansion Gap: Leave 5–10mm clearance at run ends. Copper expands when hot; do NOT inject expanding foam.
  • 🛑 Service Zones: Install removable magnetic access panels (min 150x150mm) over every tee, elbow, and valve location.

Retaining Future Access

Never permanently seal or plaster over push-fit joints. If an O-ring weeps in the future, access must be possible without demolishing skirting boards or finished flooring.
