Stage 9: Testing and Refill

Next →
Approved method required: Use the boiler manufacturer instructions and the qualified engineer's agreed process for filling, pressure testing, venting, treatment and commissioning. Do not adopt a generic pressure, duration, product or dose from this page.

Purpose

Document the approved refill and testing method, inspect the accessible installation during the agreed observation period, and record any issue before concealing pipework.

More detail on testing approach

The agreed method may involve cold and hot checks, pressure measurements, visual inspection and targeted leak detection (for example using tissue). Use this stage to record how testing was carried out and what was observed, rather than to impose a single set of numbers.

Testing record

More detail on observation period

Agree with the engineer how long the system should be observed after testing, and at what times or conditions it should be checked. Use the notes to record dates, times, any small issues and how they were resolved. Boxing should wait until this agreed period has passed without unresolved concerns.

Complete when: Testing and refill completed to the approved method, with no leaks observed.
