Route measurement

Record the actual proposed flow and return routes on site so a plumber can review the schedule before material quantities or pipe sizes are finalised.

Completion: All routes recorded and ready for review.

Do not rely on a plan or estimated totals for ordering. Record changes of level, offsets, tails, fittings, access constraints and any uncertainty for review.

Route-recording sequence

1Mark each proposed route segment and radiator connection on the site sketch.
2Measure each segment on site, including vertical changes and wall penetrations.
3Record flow and return routes separately where their paths differ.
4Note valve centres, clearances, obstacles and proposed support locations.
5Flag every unconfirmed boiler interface, pipe size, fitting or route decision for review.
6Prepare a schedule for plumber review; only then agree allowances and materials.

Review note

The route drawing is a concept. The final pipe size, material schedule, valve arrangement and fittings must be confirmed for the installed system.

Useful items

Tape measure, notebook or printable sketch, pencil, straightedge and camera for a visual record.
