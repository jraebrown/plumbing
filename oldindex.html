🛠️ 10-Stage ADHD Interactive Workflow

Stage 1: Pre-Work Approvals & Plumber Brief
🟢 DIY-Friendly (1–2 hrs)

🎯 Goal: Secure written leasehold/insurer approval and plumber verification of the 15mm plan.

  • Print review summary and floor plan.
  • Obtain written freeholder consent and notify building insurer.
  • Have plumber verify 15mm pipe sizing and stub identification strategy on site.
⚠️ Watch Out: Do NOT buy pipe or fittings before written plumber sign-off!
💡 Stage 1 Deep-Dive & Source Text
Because this is a first-floor flat above another dwelling, water leaks represent a critical liability risk. Obtain written consent from the freeholder or managing agent and notify building insurers before any work begins. Present the provisional 15mm two-pipe schematic to your heating engineer to verify radiator outputs, pump capacity, and pipe sizing before purchasing any materials. The Glow-worm documentation identifies separate central-heating flow and return connections at the boiler; the engineer must confirm how those appliance connections transition to the existing external pipework.
Stage 2: Route Measurement & Physical Sizing
🟢 DIY-Friendly (2 hrs)

🎯 Goal: Measure parallel 15mm flow and return runs across all rooms with tape measure.

  • Measure boiler drop: 1.2m (×2 = 2.4m).
  • Measure Kitchen: 2.7m (×2 = 5.4m). Reception Room: 4.9m + 1.5m (×2 = 12.8m).
  • Measure Bedroom 1: 1.5m + 2.1m (×2 = 7.2m). Bedroom 2: 2.5m (×2 = 5.0m).
  • Add branch tails (~2.0m) and wall penetrations (~1.8m).
⚠️ Watch Out: Measure site vertical drops and valve centers; do not rely on room dimensions alone!
💡 Stage 2 Deep-Dive & Source Text
Total One-Way Route: ~22.8m. Total Both Pipes (Flow + Return): ~45.6m. With 10% cutting waste allowance: ~50m. Recommended Purchase Order: 17 × 3m lengths = 51m total rigid 15mm copper pipe.
Stage 3: Safe System Draining & Isolation
🟡 DIY if Careful (1.5 hrs)

🎯 Goal: Isolate electrical power, cool boiler <30°C, attach hose, and safely drain system.

  • Switch off boiler fused spur and confirm dead with voltage tester.
  • Allow water to cool below 30°C. Attach hose to lowest drain point.
  • Open bleed valves starting from top radiator working down.
⚠️ Watch Out: Black magnetite sludge ruins carpets—protect floor coverings with wet vacuum & towels!
💡 Stage 3 Deep-Dive & Source Text
Electrical isolation prevents the boiler pump or burner from firing while dry. Draining from the lowest point creates a siphon effect. Opening bleed valves breaks vacuum locks.
Stage 4: Cable Scanning & Wall Drilling
🟢 DIY-Friendly (3 hrs)

🎯 Goal: Scan partition walls with Bosch Truvo, drill sleeved penetrations, and fit clips @ 600mm.

  • Scan BOTH sides of partition walls for hidden electric cables/metal studs.
  • Drill wall penetrations (hole size confirmed with plumber based on sleeve/insulation).
  • Install rubber-lined cushioned 15mm clips every 600mm along horizontal runs.
⚠️ Watch Out: Never drill near sockets without scanning both wall faces!
💡 Stage 4 Deep-Dive & Source Text
Confirm required wall-penetration diameter and sleeve method with the plumber. Spacing cushioned rubber-lined clips every 600mm absorbs thermal expansion movement and eliminates ticking noises.
Stage 5: Push-Fit Assembly — "The Golden Sequence"
🟡 DIY if Careful (4 hrs)

🎯 Goal: Assemble 15mm main trunks using the strict 5-step push-fit procedure.

Step 1: CUT SQUARE — Rothenberger 15mm Pipeslice (Never use a hacksaw!)
Step 2: DEBURR — Ream internal & external burrs smooth with deburring tool
Step 3: CLEAN & INSPECT — Wipe pipe end; remove swarf and debris
Step 4: MARK 23mm DEPTH — Permanent marker depth line @ ~23mm
Step 5: PUSH TO MARK — Push firmly into Tectite Sprint collar until line meets socket rim
⚠️ Watch Out: Hacksaws leave ragged edges that slice internal O-rings, causing leaks!
💡 Stage 5 Deep-Dive & Source Text
Tectite Sprint push-fit fittings use a stainless steel grab ring and EPDM O-ring seal. Clean, deburred pipe and accurate 23mm depth marking ensure the O-ring seals properly without being nicked.
Stages 6 & 7: Radiator Branch Connections
🟡 DIY if Careful (5 hrs)

🎯 Goal: Connect R1 & R2 using 15×15×15mm equal tees; terminate R3 using 90° elbows.

  • Fit 15×15×15mm equal tees on main flow/return trunks for Radiator #1 (Reception) and Radiator #2 (Bed 1).
  • Fit 90° terminal elbows on flow/return trunks for Radiator #3 (Bed 2, end of circuit).
  • Mount TRVs on flow side (provisional — confirm orientation with engineer on site).
⚠️ Watch Out: Do NOT connect radiators in series! Each radiator must bridge independently.
💡 Stage 6 & 7 Deep-Dive & Source Text
In a two-pipe system, each radiator acts as a parallel bridge. Main trunks carry hot water forward. At Radiator #3 in Bedroom 2 (furthest point), no continuation tees are used—90° elbows turn the flow trunk up into the TRV and return trunk up into the lockshield valve.
Stage 8: Boiler Underside Reconnection
🔴 Plumber Supervised (2 hrs)

🎯 Goal: Connect 15mm flow and return trunks to verified boiler stubs using Tectite couplers.

  • Plumber positively identifies CH Flow & Return stubs on site.
  • Deburr boiler stubs and mark 23mm insertion depth.
  • Push 15mm Tectite Sprint equal couplers home to connect new trunks.
⚠️ Watch Out: Do NOT touch gas (yellow handle) or DHW stubs. Do NOT cut pipes based on diagrams alone!
💡 Stage 8 Deep-Dive & Source Text
The heating engineer must positively identify the boiler's central-heating flow and return connections using the verified appliance manual, physical pipe tracing, and temperature/flow checks. Homeowner will NOT cut gas or flue connections.
Stage 9: Pressure Testing & Chemical Dosing
🔴 Plumber Supervised (2.5 hrs)

🎯 Goal: Cold/hot 1.5 bar pressure test, blue roll tissue leak check, bleed, and dose ADEY MC1 inhibitor.

  • Refit cleaned BoilerMag magnetic filter canister.
  • Plumber conducts cold hydrostatic pressure test (1.0–1.5 bar).
  • Wipe every push-fit joint and valve with dry blue roll tissue paper to detect microscopic dampness.
  • Refill system, bleed all radiators, and dose ADEY MC1 inhibitor per BS 7593.
⚠️ Watch Out: Zero boxing-in until tissue paper stays 100% dry cold AND hot!
💡 Stage 9 Deep-Dive & Source Text
Blue roll tissue paper instantly turns dark when touching even a microscopic drop of water. Testing cold and hot ensures thermal expansion doesn't induce weeping at joints. Inhibitor prevents internal corrosion.
Stage 10: Removable Boxing-In & Access Panels
🟢 DIY-Friendly (4–5 hrs)

🎯 Goal: Fit timber battens, 6mm MDF cover strips, and magnetic access hatches over every fitting.

  • Fit 25×38mm timber battens above and below insulated 15mm parallel pipes.
  • Screw 6mm MDF cover strips to battens (leaving 5–10mm end expansion gaps).
  • Cut removable access hatches over all tees, elbows, and couplers.
⚠️ Watch Out: NEVER permanently glue or seal over fittings! Always leave removable access hatches.
💡 Stage 10 Deep-Dive & Source Text
Removable access hatches ensure that if a fitting ever needs maintenance, it can be inspected and serviced without destroying wall finishes or MDF panels.
