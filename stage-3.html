03

Safe draining

Use this as a personal preparation and record page. A competent person should confirm the system-specific isolation, cooling, drainage and disposal method before work begins.

Completion: System drained safely and ready for approved work.

Hot water, stored energy, electrical equipment and contaminated heating water can cause injury or damage. Stop and obtain help if isolation, the drain route or the system condition is uncertain.

Preparation and record sequence

1Obtain the agreed method and identify the appropriate competent person responsible for isolation and draining.
2Allow the system to cool fully and protect the work area and drain route.
3Confirm the proposed drainage point, hose route, containment and disposal arrangements before opening anything.
4Follow the approved method for isolation, venting and draining; do not rely on this page as operating instructions.
5Record leaks, debris, corrosion, unusual water condition or inaccessible valves for review.
6Confirm the system condition and work area are safe before the next approved stage.

Boundary

Boiler electrical and gas-related work, system isolation and reconnection must be completed or directed by the appropriately qualified person in line with the appliance instructions and applicable requirements.

Personal checklist 0%

Useful items

Absorbent protection, suitable containment and a way to document observations. The competent person specifies any system-specific equipment.
