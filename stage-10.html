Stage 10 of 10: Boxing In
010
Boxing In
⏱ 4-5 hrs Medium
🎯 Stage Goal:

Mount timber battens, fit 6mm MDF cover panels along skirting, and create removable access hatches over every fitting.

⚠️ Watch-out:
NEVER permanently glue or seal push-fit joints inside boxing without removable access panels! Leave 5-10mm end expansion gaps.
✅ Complete When:
Pipework neatly boxed in with timber battens and 6mm MDF, removable access hatches over all joints, and 5-10mm thermal expansion gaps left.
⚠️ ACCESS HATCH MANDATE: Building regulations and maintenance standards require every push-fit fitting to remain accessible. Never permanently plaster or glue over joints!

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Fit 25x38mm sawn timber battens above and below insulated 15mm parallel pipes along wall perimeters.
2
Cut 6mm MDF sheets into ~80mm wide cover strips.
3
Screw MDF strips to timber battens (leaving 5-10mm gap at ends of runs for copper thermal expansion).
4
Create magnetic or screw-removable access hatches directly over every tee, elbow, and coupler fitting.
5
Apply decorator caulk along top edge and paint MDF to match existing skirting boards.
🔨 Thermal Gap: Leave a 5–10mm clearance gap at each end of the MDF boxing run. Copper pipe expands when heated to 60°C and will buckle if boxed too tightly.
