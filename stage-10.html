Stage 10: Boxing In

Index →
Low priority, last step: Do not box in pipework until the agreed monitoring period is complete and any defects are resolved. Design boxing around the actual installation and retain access required for inspection, servicing and future repair.

Purpose

Finish the appearance of proven pipe runs without hiding joints or components that need future access. Materials, clearances, access-panel positions and fixing method should suit the measured route rather than a prescribed generic detail.

More detail on boxing design

Measure from finished surfaces to pipe centres and joints, then plan boxing that gives enough clearance for insulation and movement without crushing the pipework. Decide where removable panels or hatches will go before you start fixing materials.

Personal check

More detail on access

Think in terms of “service zones”: note where isolation valves, radiator tails, tees, filters or other components are, and make sure boxing can be removed or opened at those points without demolition. Record the locations and any fixings that need to be undone.

Complete when: Boxing completed, with required access retained.
