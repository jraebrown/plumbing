Stage 5: Push-Fit Preparation

Next →
PLANNING DOCUMENT — Follow the current instructions supplied with the exact VSH Tectite Sprint fitting and copper tube. Do not rely on this page as a substitute for manufacturer instructions or engineer approval.
Safety-critical: A square, clean, undamaged and fully deburred tube end is essential to protect the fitting seal. Stop if the tube is distorted, deeply scratched, contaminated or cannot be prepared exactly as the manufacturer requires.

Preparation sequence

  1. Cut square. Use a method that produces a square, clean cut and is approved by the fitting manufacturer. A quality tube cutter is the preferred planning assumption; do not use a cut that leaves damage or uncontrolled burrs.
  2. Deburr inside and outside. Remove burrs carefully with a suitable reamer so that no sharp edge can damage the fitting seal.
  3. Clean and inspect. Use only the cleaning/preparation method permitted by the fitting manufacturer. Remove all swarf, residue and loose debris; do not leave abrasive fibres in or on the tube.
  4. Mark insertion depth. Use the insertion-depth requirement printed in the current manufacturer instructions or fitting packaging. The previous 23 mm figure is a planning reference only and must be checked for the exact fitting.
  5. Push fully home and check. Insert the tube fully to the marked depth, then visually check the mark and joint in accordance with the fitting instructions. Do not force a joint that does not assemble normally.

Before proceeding

Confirm the exact fitting range, tube compatibility, pressure/temperature ratings, insertion depth and any demounting or inspection requirements from current manufacturer information.
