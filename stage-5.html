Stage 5 of 10: Push-Fit Golden Sequence
05
Push-Fit Golden Sequence
⏱ 4 hrs Medium-High
🎯 Stage Goal:

Prepare 15mm copper tube ends and assemble Tectite Sprint push-fit main trunks using the strict 5-step Golden Sequence.

⚠️ Watch-out:
NEVER use a hacksaw! Hacksaws leave ragged copper burrs that slice internal push-fit O-rings, causing immediate leaks.
✅ Complete When:
Every 15mm joint assembled using clean, deburred pipe pushed firmly home to the 23mm insertion depth mark.
🚨 THE MOST CRITICAL STAGE: Skipping deburring or depth marking is the #1 cause of push-fit joint failure. Un-deburred pipe edges WILL tear O-rings!

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
STEP 1: CUT SQUARE — Cut 15mm copper tube squarely using the Rothenberger 15mm Pipeslice. (Never use a hacksaw!).
2
STEP 2: DEBURR — Ream internal and external burrs smooth with a deburring tool.
3
STEP 3: CLEAN & INSPECT — Clean tube end with fine steel wool grade 00; remove all swarf, grit, and dust.
4
STEP 4: MARK 23mm DEPTH — Mark permanent insertion depth line at exactly 23mm from pipe end.
5
STEP 5: PUSH TO MARK — Push pipe firmly into Tectite Sprint fitting until line meets socket collar; verify grab ring holds.
📏 23mm Insertion Rule: For 15mm VSH Tectite Sprint fittings, full insertion depth is exactly 23mm. Always mark before pushing!
