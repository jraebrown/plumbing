PLANNING DOCUMENT — Follow the current instructions supplied with the exact VSH Tectite Sprint fitting and compatible copper tube. This page is not a substitute for manufacturer instructions or engineer approval.
Safety-critical: A square, clean, undamaged and fully deburred tube end is essential to protect the fitting seal. Stop if the tube is distorted, deeply scratched, contaminated or cannot be prepared exactly as the manufacturer requires.

Preparation sequence

1Cut square. Use a method approved by the fitting manufacturer that produces a square, clean cut without damage or uncontrolled burrs.
2Deburr inside and outside. Carefully remove burrs with a suitable tool so no sharp edge can damage the fitting seal.
3Clean and inspect. Use only the manufacturer-permitted preparation method; remove swarf, residue and loose debris without leaving abrasive fibres.
4Mark insertion depth. Use the current requirement for the exact fitting from its packaging or manufacturer instructions. Any earlier figure is only a planning reference.
5Push fully home and check. Insert fully to the marked depth and inspect as the fitting instructions require. Do not force a joint that does not assemble normally.
