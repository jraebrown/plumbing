Pre-Work Approvals
⏱ 1-2 hrs Easy
🎯 Stage Goal:

Secure written freeholder consent, notify building insurer, and get plumber verification of the 15mm plan.

⚠️ Watch-out:
First-floor flat — any water leak affects the neighbour below! Do NOT order pipe or fittings until the plumber approves the route.
✅ Complete When:
Leasehold consent confirmed, insurer notified in writing, Glow-worm 24c manual downloaded, and plumber signs off on the 15mm parallel route.
🚨 CRITICAL RISK: First-floor flat location — leak affects neighbour below!

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Check your flat lease agreement — verify permissions regarding plumbing alterations.
2
Email your freeholder or managing agent with the provisional 15mm two-pipe proposal.
3
Notify your building and contents insurer in writing before commencing any works.
4
Download and print the official Glow-worm Compact 24c-AS/1 installation and servicing manual.
5
Label existing boiler stubs provisionally and note that physical identification is required on site.
6
Have your qualified heating engineer (plumber) review and verify the 15mm parallel route strategy.
7
Do NOT order pipe or fittings until written plumber sign-off is complete!
📋 Mandatory Document Pack: Printed Floor Plan, Glow-worm Manual, Leasehold Consent Letter, Insurer Notification Email.
