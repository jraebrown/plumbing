Stage 7 of 10: Radiator #3 Terminal
07
Radiator #3 Terminal
⏱ 2 hrs Medium
🎯 Stage Goal:

Terminate 15mm flow and return trunks into Radiator #3 in Bedroom 2 using 90° terminal elbows.

⚠️ Watch-out:
Do NOT join flow and return directly together! Radiator #3 forms the final parallel bridge ending the circuit (no continuation tees).
✅ Complete When:
Radiator #3 connected via terminal 90° elbows; flow trunk terminates into TRV and return trunk starts at Lockshield.
⚠️ End of Loop Rule: Radiator #3 is the furthest point on the circuit. Do NOT fit tees here. Flow and return terminate into the radiator valves.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Route 15mm flow main trunk into Bedroom 2 to Radiator #3 left side.
2
Fit 15mm 90° terminal elbow turning flow pipe vertically up into TRV valve inlet.
3
Route 15mm return main trunk from Radiator #3 right side.
4
Fit 15mm 90° terminal elbow turning return pipe vertically down from Lockshield valve outlet.
5
Verify no continuation tees are fitted past Radiator #3 — this completes the parallel circuit loop.
↪️ Fitting Specs: Tectite Sprint 15mm 90° Elbows (Screwfix Code: 6486G). Requires 12 units total across corners and terminals.
